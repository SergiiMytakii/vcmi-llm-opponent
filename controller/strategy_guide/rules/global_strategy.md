# Global strategy

## When useful
Choose or materially reconsider the saved victory course.

## Facts and unknowns
Read victory conditions, map coverage, bases, forces, income and native routes. Known coordinates do not establish reachability; omitted objects and enemy intentions remain unknown.

## Alternatives to compare
Compare 2-3 distinct supported paths to victory by decisive gain, days required, preparation and loss risk. Prefer the shortest credible path that retains a viable winning force. Do not invent options or exact victory dates.

## Benefits, costs and risks
Keep one course with 3-6 measurable stages. Each preparation stage names the offensive step it unlocks and a review point. Compare acting now with waiting: what improves, what the enemy can gain, and when the next operation becomes feasible. More troops or gold are useful only when they advance victory.

## When to reconsider
Review changed threats, loss, blockage or stalled stages. Attach no_progress to unfinished conquest, not only army growth. strategy_stalls measures delay; compare a faster supported operation before renewing preparation. Revise the course when its direction no longer serves victory.

## Executor limits
Native state and receipts confirm progress. Historical capture is not current ownership. Quotes remain conditional; supporting defense does not complete conquest. Keep operative plans within 3-7 days and use the existing reply contract.
