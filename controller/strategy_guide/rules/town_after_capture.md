# Town after capture

## When useful
Choose departure, return or renewed protection after capture or a completed hold.

## Facts and unknowns
Read actual income, stock/growth, buildings, funds, army pools, threats, delivery/return routes and remaining bases. Separate the garrison from the field army; purchases and conditional returns do not prove lasting safety.

## Alternatives to compare
- Compare departure, funded garrison, interception and holding.
- Leave enough stationary force to deter observed weak raiders while preserving the strike army.
- For a base trade, compare income, recruitment, fortifications and development time. Unknown benefits of the new base do not offset confirmed losses of the main base. The trade should shorten the path to victory.

## Benefits, costs and risks
- Before renewing a hold, compare cumulative delay with the next offensive operation. Name the gain already obtained, why it did not unlock departure and what the next wait buys.
- A new deadline, lost helper or weekly refresh alone adds no benefit. When preparation repeatedly fails, change its method or operation instead of renewing the same promise.

Value the town's recurring income and affordable troop production against development, holding and delivery costs. Taking an enemy town may both strengthen own production and deny theirs; use observed facts, with future benefits conditional. Choose supported development, reinforcement or continued conquest that converts the capture into military advantage.

## When to reconsider
Recheck after stock, force, routes or ownership change, including after helper loss/hiring. After the exit condition changes compare options again. A completed hold needs a new operation; use strategy_stalls to compare preparation with executable progress.

## Executor limits
Use prepare_garrison/intercept_hero contracts. Keep required stationary protection through the absence and re-quote attacks after allocation. Keeping the full main needs a separate purchase or an explanation why preparation cannot succeed. Populate defense_exit under the common output contract.
