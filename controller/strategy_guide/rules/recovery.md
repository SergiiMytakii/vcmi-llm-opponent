# Recovery

## When useful
After acknowledged battle loss, commander loss or depleted mana constraining an operation.

## Facts and unknowns
Check commander_options: hero_multiplier, hero_combat_value, mana, movement and sources/meetings. Check mana mechanics in mana_magic when unclear.

## Alternatives to compare
- First recover an offered own retreated/surrendered hero before its tavern slot is replaced. Compare skills, equipment and legal funded hiring gates.
- Then compare safe capture, reinforcement then dependent capture, preservation and purposeful scouting from the newly observed state.
- With unknown follow-up quotes, make funded reinforcement the concrete task and reassess after handoff; helpers may scout/collect.

## Benefits, costs and risks
- Rehire preserves development/artifacts but retreat does not restore the lost army. Budget recovery time, funds and exposure.
- Transfers must preserve affected_operations: continue the target with a suitable commander or explain preparation/abandonment.
- A holder change alone does not justify replacing conquest with scouting or stripping a stronger commander for an unrelated chain.

## When to reconsider
Review commander loss, failed delivery, changed target or missing source. Complete recovery only after confirmed handoff and fresh next-route validation.

## Executor limits
Use current hiring, delivery and preservation contracts. Urgent native recovery still needs a funded legal slot.
