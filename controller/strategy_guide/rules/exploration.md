# Exploration

## When useful
When conquest lacks routes, new information may enable an operation, or scouting exposure/loss needs reassessment.

## Facts and unknowns
Check offered viewpoints, unvisited scholar/chest/obelisk sites, keymaster tents, border guards/gates and subterranean passages. For key sites use key_color, key_owned, matching_visible_refs and eligible_hero_refs. Seeing a tent gives no key; visiting it unlocks matching colours for all own heroes. Use an eligible actor and a fresh native route.
Compare arrival, information gain, movement and commitments. Route losses differ from end_turn_exposure: zero planned losses proves no safety during the opponent's turn. stop_positions assume the quoted route stays unchanged. Threat distances/connections prove no enemy movement, abilities or intent; absent exposure facts mean unknown. Fog counts promise no useful objects; unseen gate exits remain unknown.

## Alternatives to compare
Give a feasible matching border opening high priority when it may reveal a new region or enable an objective. Use a suitable helper to collect the key, then re-quote routes after confirmation. Compare useful sites, another viewpoint, shorter route, protection, subterranean gates and onward operations through learned passages. Prefer progress to repeating explored entrances after an old route fails; returning needs a follow-up purpose. Safe collection along offered roads must preserve the main's supported operation. Roads reveal no hidden town or safety.

## Benefits, costs and risks
Information may unlock routes but costs movement and exposure. Site contents and gate exit danger remain uncertain. A visible gate alone does not justify risking a critical defender/main. After scout loss require a concrete new gain, route or protection before repeating; cheap hiring alone is insufficient. Compare existing helpers with replacement and preserve funded commitments. Never infer guaranteed interception or use unseen enemies.

## When to reconsider
After a key, border visit or crossing compare new offered routes/targets; after scout loss review exposure. Revise commitments when new evidence justifies it.

## Executor limits
Use the current scout_area, visit_site, scout_frontier and explore_passage contracts, offered arrivals and native receipts. Advice supplies no missing routes or guarantees.
