# Exploration

## When useful
When conquest lacks routes, frontiers are blocked, new information may enable an operation, or scouting exposure/loss requires reassessment.

## Facts and unknowns
Check scouting_options, visible unvisited scholar/chest/obelisk sites, gates and observed_passages. Compare the actor's offered arrival, expected information gain, movement and commitments. Assess route losses separately from end_turn_exposure: zero planned losses proves no safety during the opponent's turn. stop_positions assume the quoted route remains unchanged. Visible threat distances/connections prove no enemy movement, abilities or intentions; absent exposure facts mean unknown. Fog counts promise no useful objects; unexplored gates establish no exit or onward route.

## Alternatives to compare
Compare another viewpoint, shorter route, protection or waiting with a concrete reconnaissance gain worth exposure. Also compare useful sites, frontier scouting, a visible gate and onward operations through learned passages. Prefer useful onward progress to repeating an explored entrance after an old surface route fails; returning through a learned passage needs a follow-up purpose. Use suitable helpers for safe collection along offered roads when this preserves the main's supported operation. Roads reveal no hidden town/safety.

## Benefits, costs and risks
Information may unlock targets/routes but costs movement and actor exposure. Unseen site contents and gate exit danger remain uncertain. Avoid risking a critical defender/main merely because a gate is visible. After scout loss explain what new information, route, protection or concrete gain justifies a similar attempt; cheap hiring alone does not. Compare using existing helpers with replacement and preserve funded commitments. Never infer guaranteed interception or use unseen enemies.

## When to reconsider
After confirmed visit/crossing compare new frontiers/targets; after scout loss review exposure before replacement. Reassess changed routes, useful objectives, defense needs or preservation conflicts; explicitly revise a commitment if the new operation justifies it.

## Executor limits
Follow the current scout_area, visit_site, scout_frontier and explore_passage contracts with offered arrivals and native confirmations. The always-present instructions own completion, visibility, permitted passages and army/loss/reserve limits; advice supplies no missing routes or guarantees.
