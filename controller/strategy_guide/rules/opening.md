# Opening

## When useful
At the start of a campaign or before a settled operation; use circumstances, not a universal day number.

## Facts and unknowns
Check own armies, commander capability, offered routes, income, funds, building options and visible threats. Hidden objectives and enemy intent remain unknown.

## Alternatives to compare
Compare supported expansion by a viable main; useful independent scouting; income or troop production; saving funds for demonstrated defense. Early on, prioritize reachable sawmills and ore mines when wood or ore limits construction; weigh route risk and existing income.

## Benefits, costs and risks
Expansion gains assets with battle exposure. Scouting costs movement and exposure. Construction spends shared funds and can delay recruitment. A new hero needs a concrete uncovered defense, reinforcement or collection job; cash and starting troops alone are no job. Prioritize a supported mine when its known resource output addresses the next funded bottleneck; an unknown subtype cannot justify that claim.

## When to reconsider
Reassess after discovery of a useful target, route changes, income loss, a threatened last town or confirmed delivery.

## Executor limits
Use only supported goals and current offered routes. An opening schedule, future building or intended battle proves no readiness or execution.
