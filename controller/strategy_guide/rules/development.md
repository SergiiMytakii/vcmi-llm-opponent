# Development

## When useful
When income, production, recruitment or hero readiness constrains the next operation.

## Facts and unknowns
Check resources, income, quoted construction/recruitment costs, resource_calendar, stack compatibility, source/meeting options and safe mines/sites. Future income and delivery are uncertain until observed.

## Alternatives to compare
Compare economic construction, troop production, supported mine capture, reinforcement, useful hero visits and retaining funds for defense.

## Benefits, costs and risks
Income pays over time; troop production changes later options; current recruitment improves available force but may still need delivery. Mines/sites cost movement and may expose armies. Parallel alternatives cannot jointly spend the same stock or funds. Compare the quoted prerequisite chain, total cost and construction days with the next army need. Evaluate income payback only from known income_delta and costs; a long payback may lose useful expansion or defense time. Compare production before the next growth with affordable recruitment and delivery, using the current resource_calendar rather than assuming new dwellings change forecast growth.

## When to reconsider
Reassess after income loss, new threats, actual delivery, stock changes, a new route or confirmation that an economic delay harms the operation.

## Executor limits
Buildings need offered supported IDs. Reinforcement requires legal current delivery and its receipt; buying stock does not establish arrival. Do not invent future recruitment routes.
