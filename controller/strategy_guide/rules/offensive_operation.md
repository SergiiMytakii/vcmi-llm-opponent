# Offensive operation

## When useful
Select a conquest operation or reassess a position changed by a passage opening, capture, substantial losses or reinforcement.

## Facts and unknowns
Check offensive_preparation, current/reinforced arrivals, sources/meetings, commander capabilities, siege losses, retained floors and follow-up routes.

## Alternatives to compare
- Compare 2-4 relevant continuations: attack, expansion, reinforcement then attack, or protection. Use concrete targets and actors; omit options without a useful purpose.
- For each, outline the next 2-3 steps: benefit names the continuation and winning gain; cost gives days, cumulative losses, remaining force and exposed bases; uncertainty gives a plausible enemy response and unverified follow-up.
- Compare enemy-main attacks and town captures before minor targets. Chase weaker heroes for a base threat, obstruction or negligible detour; explain bypassing an offered town.
- Compare acting now with reinforcement then attack. Prefer feasible courier delivery over returning the main.

## Benefits, costs and risks
- Choose the shortest credible winning continuation, including enemy gains during delay. Expansion must repay its travel and holding costs through usable troops or income.
- A costly full assault does not rule out a supported intermediate breakthrough or position. Compare its own losses, enemy reply and useful next step; missing quotes leave feasibility unknown.
- Use the strongest viable strike army. A weaker commander needs a concrete advantage; compare reported mana/combat capabilities as well as force value.
- Helpers scout, collect or provide limited defense. Preserve the operation their work supports.

## When to reconsider
After a changed position, compare continuations before extending the old operation. Recompare actors after losses/transfers; check arrival, force, readiness and home protection. Set a review at the next decisive result; future steps stay conditional until freshly quoted.

## Executor limits
Use explicit reinforcement dependencies and fresh admission quotes. Keep policy.max_loss_ratio as the ordinary limit; goal.risk may authorize higher losses for a named decisive attack with a strategic justification (endgame), not merely to bypass a blocked quote.
