# Offensive operation

## When useful
Select or continue a conquest operation, including actor changes after losses/transfers.

## Facts and unknowns
Check offensive_preparation, current/reinforced arrivals, sources/meetings, commander capabilities, losses, retained floors and follow-up routes.

## Alternatives to compare
- Compare enemy-main attacks and town captures before minor targets; include combined army/base removal and siege losses.
- Chase weaker heroes for a concrete base threat, obstruction or negligible detour. Explain bypassing an offered town.
- Compare reinforcement then dependent attack with acting now. Prefer feasible courier delivery over returning the main.

## Benefits, costs and risks
- Evaluate the whole operation: gain, days, cumulative losses, exposed assets and next feasible step. A cheap first fight is insufficient.
- Use the strongest viable strike army. A weaker commander needs a concrete advantage; compare reported mana/combat capabilities as well as force value.
- Helpers scout, collect or provide limited defense. Preserve the operation their work supports.

## When to reconsider
After substantial losses/transfers compare actors for the same target: arrival, losses, remaining force, combat readiness and home protection. Keep/switch for a current advantage, not prior commitment. Recheck duties, reserves and routes.

## Executor limits
Use explicit reinforcement dependencies and fresh admission quotes. Keep policy.max_loss_ratio as the ordinary limit; goal.risk may authorize higher losses for a named decisive attack with a strategic justification (endgame), not merely to bypass a blocked quote.
