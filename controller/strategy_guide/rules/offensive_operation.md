# Offensive operation

## When useful
When selecting the next conquest objective or continuing an offensive operation.

## Facts and unknowns
Check offensive_preparation, current versus reinforced arrivals, source/meeting options, commander strength, losses, reserve floors and the follow-up route. Unknown meetings or land beyond a frontier prove no onward attack.

## Alternatives to compare
Compare supported attacks on the strongest observed enemy army and enemy-town captures, especially its last known town, before minor targets. Prefer removing the main force or denying its recruitment base when feasible; if both coincide in a defended town, compare that combined gain and siege losses. Retreat restrictions require reported rules, not assumptions. Chase weak heroes for a concrete base threat, route obstruction or negligible detour; compare the delay against major objectives. If bypassing an offered town, give a concrete operational reason.

## Benefits, costs and risks
Conquest gains income and initiative at the cost of travel and losses. Compare losses across successive fights: a profitable first capture can leave no viable force for the next. Preserve useful combat capabilities when reported; equal nominal army values need not imply equal commanders or combat options. Reinforcement costs time/funds but may unlock the target. Scouting is useful when capture needs information. Use the strongest viable strike army; helpers collect/scout/provide limited defense. A weaker main needs a specific operational reason and a currently executable operation; unused movement alone is no reason.

## When to reconsider
After each battle, capture or changed route, compare fresh major objectives before renewing a pursuit or extending its deadline. Substantial reinforcement may unlock a better attack; an existing goal alone gives no reason to keep it.

## Executor limits
For future attacks, use reinforce_hero with named recipient, final army and deadline, then explicit dependencies. Prefer supported courier delivery over returning the main. Compare offered frontier routes and losses over the operation horizon; never raise loss policy merely to unblock an attack.
